## First paragraph

```
Most slow or clogged sink, tub and shower drains clear with one of three things, in this order: a kettle of boiling water, a plunger with the overflow hole covered, and a hand auger fed into the pipe and twisted to catch the hair and soap. Skip the chemical cleaner; it works by heat and can damage older pipes. If more than one drain is slow at the same time, the clog is in your main line, and that is when to call us.
```
