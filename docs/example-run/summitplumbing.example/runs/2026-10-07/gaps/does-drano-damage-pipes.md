## First paragraph

```
Occasional use of a chemical drain cleaner on a slow bathroom sink will not hurt a modern PVC drain, but repeated use, or any use on a fully blocked pipe, can. The product works by generating heat, and when it sits in a pipe that is not draining, that heat and the caustic liquid work on the pipe and its joints instead of the clog.
```
