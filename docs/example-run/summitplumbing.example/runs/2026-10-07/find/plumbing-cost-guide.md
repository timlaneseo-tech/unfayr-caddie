## Why it sits here

The H1 asks the right question, but the first dollar figure on the page is in a table two-thirds of the way down, after six paragraphs about how pricing works; the hourly rate 2,350 people a month search for is a table row with no heading, and the meta description ("Honest pricing, no surprises") contains no price at all. Gemini answers "how much does a plumber cost" with HomeAdvisor's and Forbes' national ranges because nothing on this page states the Boise range in a sentence anyone can quote, even though the numbers are here.

## Fixes

### 1. Title

Before:
```
Plumbing Cost Guide | Summit Plumbing
```
After:
```
Plumber Cost in Boise: Hourly Rates and Typical Prices
```
Why: "how much does a plumber cost", "plumber hourly rate", "plumber cost per hour" and "plumber prices" all appear in the first six words; 54 characters, brand dropped because the page competes with national cost guides and the words matter more here.

## How much does it cost to replace a water heater in Boise?

Replacing a 40 to 50 gallon tank water heater costs $1,600 to $2,800 installed in the Boise area, and a tankless installation runs $3,500 to $6,500 because of the venting and gas line work. We quote the exact figure in writing after seeing the unit.

### 6. FAQ

Add before "Get a Quote":

```
## Common questions about plumber prices

**How much does it cost to unclog a drain?**
Clearing a clogged drain runs $150 to $350 at Summit Plumbing. Main sewer line cleaning and hydro jetting are quoted after a camera inspection.

**How much does it cost to repipe a house?**
A whole-house repipe in PEX for a two-bathroom home runs $6,000 to $14,000. Older North End homes with galvanized pipe tend toward the top of that range.

**How much does a plumber charge to fix a leak?**
Fixing an accessible leaking pipe costs $150 to $450. Leaks inside walls or under slabs cost more because of the access work, and we quote them after seeing the spot.

**Why are plumbers so expensive?**
You are paying for a licensed tradesperson with a four-year apprenticeship, liability insurance, a bonded license, a stocked truck and the judgement to find which of a dozen possible causes is the real one. That is why a fifteen-minute fix can still cost a couple of hundred dollars, and why the cheapest quote is often not the cheapest job.

**How much is a plumber visit?**
Our diagnostic visit is $89, and it is credited to the repair if you go ahead. After-hours and weekend visits add $150.
```
