## Why it sits here

Neither the title nor the H1 names a city, so the 5,200 "near me" and "24 hour plumber" searches a month see a generic "Emergency Plumber" against competitors whose titles say where they are. The how-to questions that make up the other half of the page's impressions (shut off the main, thaw frozen pipes, burst pipe) are all answered inside one section called "Stop the Water First", a heading none of those searchers would scan for; "how to shut off water main" slid from 3.5 to 6.5 this period, which is what happens when a page answers a question without a heading that says so.

## Fixes

### 1. Title

Before:
```
Emergency Plumber | Summit Plumbing
```
After:
```
24-Hour Emergency Plumber in Boise | Summit Plumbing
```
Why: covers "emergency plumber near me", "24 hour plumber near me" and "24 hour plumber" with the city Google needs to match "near me"; 52 characters.

### 2. Meta description

Before:
```
24/7 emergency plumber in Boise. Burst pipes, sewage backups, no water, gas smells. Summit Plumbing answers the phone day and night.
```
After:
```
Burst pipe, frozen pipes, sewage backup or no water? Summit's emergency plumber answers 24/7 in Boise, Meridian and Nampa, usually on site within 90 minutes.
```
Why: adds the response time the page already promises and the two suburbs with their own queries; 157 characters.

### 3. Rename "Stop the Water First" as the question

Before:
```
## Stop the Water First

After:
```
## Where is my water shut-off valve, and how do I turn off the main?

Why: the paragraph under it (currently paragraph 4) already answers both questions in order; "how to shut off water main" (6.5, down from 3.5) and "where is my water shut off valve" (8.9) just need a heading in their words. 67 characters.

### 4. New section after the shut-off section

Add this heading and paragraph directly after the burst-pipe paragraph (currently paragraph 5, "For a burst pipe, shut off the main..."), before "Emergency Pricing":

```
## How do I thaw frozen pipes?

Open the faucet the frozen pipe feeds, then warm the pipe gradually from the faucet end back toward the frozen section with a hair dryer or hot towels. Never use an open flame. If water is already running down a wall or across a floor, the pipe has burst: shut off the main, open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the lines, and call us.
```
Why: "how to thaw frozen pipes" (1,595 impressions) and "frozen pipes what to do" (1,385) are the page's largest non-local intent and are currently one clause inside a paragraph about burst pipes; 68 words, every step from the existing paragraph 5.
