### 7. FAQ

Add before "Making It Last Longer":

```
## Common questions about water heater lifespan

**How often should you replace a water heater?**
Plan on replacing a tank water heater at ten to twelve years, or sooner if it shows rusty water, rumbling or moisture at the base. A tankless unit that is descaled every year can run about twenty.

**Can a water heater last 20 years?**
A tank heater occasionally reaches twenty years with soft water, light use and a replaced anode rod, but that is the exception. Tankless heaters reach twenty as a matter of course when they are descaled annually.

**Is it worth replacing a 15-year-old water heater?**
Usually yes. At fifteen years a tank is three to seven years past its expected life, and a repair of any size is money spent on a unit that can fail without warning and release its full tank. Replace it on your schedule rather than its own.

**Does flushing a water heater extend its life?**
Yes. Flushing once a year clears the sediment that overheats the bottom of the tank, and that overheating is what eventually cracks the steel. Pair it with an anode rod check every three years.
```
