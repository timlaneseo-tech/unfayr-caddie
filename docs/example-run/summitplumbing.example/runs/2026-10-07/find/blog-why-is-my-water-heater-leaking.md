### 5. FAQ

Add after the new "How do I turn off a leaking water heater?" section, before the closing paragraph about Summit:

```
## Common questions about a leaking water heater

**Is a leaking water heater an emergency?**
A leak from the tank itself is: shut off the water and the gas or power and call the same day, because a corroded tank can fail suddenly. A drip from a fitting or the relief valve is urgent but not an emergency; catch it in a pan and book a repair.

**Can a leaking water heater be repaired?**
Leaks from the top fittings, the temperature and pressure relief valve, the drain valve or an element gasket can all be repaired, usually in about an hour. A leak from the tank body cannot; the tank has to be replaced.

**Can I still use my water heater if it is leaking?**
If the leak is a slow drip from a valve or fitting, you can keep using it for a day or two with a pan underneath. If the tank itself is leaking, turn it off and stop using it until it is replaced.

**Is it a leak or just condensation?**
A cold garage can make a gas heater sweat when it fires up, and the drips stop once the tank is up to temperature. Dry the area, lay down a paper towel and check back in an hour: if it stays dry, it was condensation.
```
