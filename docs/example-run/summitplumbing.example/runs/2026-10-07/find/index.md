## Why it sits here

The home page is 158 words, and "plumber boise" has climbed from 10.6 to 6.9 on them, so something is working; the brand queries are all at position 1 and need nothing. What holds the city queries at 6 to 9 is that the title leads with the brand and the H1 ("Boise's Plumber Since 2009") is the only place "plumber" and "Boise" sit together, while the 1,400-review claim and the suburb names that would answer "best plumber in boise" and "plumber meridian idaho" are in paragraphs under headings that do not mention them.

## Fixes

### 1. Title

Before:
```
Summit Plumbing | Licensed Plumber in Boise, ID
```
After:
```
Plumber in Boise, ID | Summit Plumbing, Licensed Since 2009
```
Why: puts the service and city first for "plumber boise", "boise plumber" and "plumbers boise idaho" (5,700 impressions a month) and keeps the brand, which still wins its own 2,000 searches at position 1; 59 characters.

### 2. Meta description

Before:
```
Summit Plumbing is a licensed, family-owned plumber serving Boise, Meridian, Eagle, and Nampa since 2009. Water heaters, drains, repairs, and 24/7 emergency service.
```
After:
```
Family-owned Boise plumber since 2009 with 1,400 five-star reviews. Water heaters, drains, repairs and 24/7 emergency service across the Treasure Valley.
```
Why: moves the review count into the snippet, where it answers "best plumber in boise" before anyone clicks; 153 characters.

### 3. Rename "Why Homeowners Choose Summit"

Before:
```
## Why Homeowners Choose Summit
```
After:
```
## Why 1,400 Boise reviews call Summit the plumber to call
```
Why: "best plumber in boise" (518 at 11.8) and "who is the best plumber in boise" want social proof under a heading they can scan for; the paragraph beneath already carries the review count and the warranty. Avoids claiming "best" in your own voice. 57 characters.

