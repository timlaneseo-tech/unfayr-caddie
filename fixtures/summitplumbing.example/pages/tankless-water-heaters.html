Tankless Water Heater Installation

A tankless water heater heats water only when you open a tap, so you never run out and you stop paying to keep forty gallons hot around the clock. Summit Plumbing installs Navien, Rinnai, and Noritz tankless units across the Treasure Valley, and we service every brand we install.

Is Tankless Right for Your Home?

Tankless makes the most sense for households that run out of hot water, homes where the tank takes up space you want back, and anyone planning to stay in the house long enough to recoup the higher installation cost through energy savings and a longer service life. It makes less sense if your gas line or electrical panel would need a major upgrade to support it, which we will tell you at the quote.

What Installation Involves

A tankless unit needs a larger gas line than most tank heaters, dedicated venting, and a condensate drain. A straightforward swap in a garage with good gas supply takes about a day. Homes that need the gas line upsized or the unit relocated take longer. We handle the permit, the inspection, and the haul-away of your old tank.

Sizing

Tankless units are sized by flow rate in gallons per minute at a given temperature rise. In Boise, incoming water in winter can be close to 40 degrees, so you need more capacity than the same house would in Arizona. A family of four running a shower and a dishwasher at the same time typically needs a unit rated around 8 to 9 gallons per minute at a 70-degree rise. We size every installation to your fixtures, not a brochure.

Maintenance

Treasure Valley water is hard enough that a tankless unit should be descaled once a year. It takes about an hour with a pump and a few gallons of white vinegar, or we can do it as part of an annual service. Skipping it is the number one reason tankless heaters fail early.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a tankless water heater cost installed?

Most tankless installations in the Boise area run between $3,500 and $6,500 including the unit, venting, permit, and labor. Gas line upgrades or relocation add to that.

How long does it take to install a tankless water heater?

A direct replacement in an accessible location is usually done in one day. Jobs that require a new gas line or electrical work can take two.

Do tankless water heaters qualify for rebates in Idaho?

Intermountain Gas and Idaho Power periodically offer rebates on qualifying high-efficiency units, and federal tax credits apply to many condensing models. We will tell you what applies at the time of your quote.

How long do tankless water heaters last?

Around twenty years with annual descaling, roughly double a conventional tank.
