About Us

Summit Plumbing was started in 2009 by Dan and Marisol Reyes with one van and a promise to quote every job in writing before starting it. Today the company has eleven licensed plumbers, a shop on Fairview Avenue, and more than 1,400 five-star reviews, but the promise has not changed.

Our Team

Every technician is a Summit employee, has passed a background check, and completed a four-year Idaho apprenticeship. We invest in training on tankless systems, trenchless sewer repair, and water quality so our customers get current answers, not guesses.

Community

We sponsor two Little League teams, donate a water heater install every winter through the Boise Rescue Mission, and offer discounts for seniors, veterans, and teachers.
