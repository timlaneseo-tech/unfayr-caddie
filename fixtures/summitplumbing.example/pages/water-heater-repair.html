Water Heater Repair in Boise

No hot water? Strange noises from the tank? Water on the garage floor? Summit Plumbing repairs gas, electric, and tankless water heaters across Boise, Meridian, Eagle, and Nampa, usually the same day you call.

Water Heater Problems We Fix

  • No hot water or not enough hot water
  • Pilot light that will not stay lit
  • Popping, rumbling, or banging noises
  • Reset button that keeps tripping
  • Water that smells like rotten eggs
  • Leaks from valves, fittings, or the tank
  • Thermostat and heating element failures
  • Tankless error codes and flow problems

Repair or Replace?

We will always tell you straight. If your heater is under eight years old and the problem is a valve, thermostat, element, or thermocouple, repair is nearly always the right call. If the tank itself is leaking, or the heater is past ten years and needs a major part, replacement usually saves money within a couple of years. We carry both options on the truck and can do either the same day.
