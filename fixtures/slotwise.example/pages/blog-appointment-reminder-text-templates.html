Appointment Reminder Text Templates

Priya Natarajan, Slotwise. Updated September 2025.

A reminder text is the cheapest thing you will ever do to protect your revenue. Businesses that send one see no-show rates fall by roughly a third, and the text itself takes four seconds to read. The hard part is not sending it; it is writing one that sounds like you, gets confirmed, and does not annoy anyone.

Below are twenty-five templates we have collected from Slotwise customers, grouped by situation. Copy the one closest to your business, swap in your details, and you are done. Every template is under 160 characters so it sends as a single SMS.

Friendly Reminders

Hi {first name}! Just a reminder about your appointment with {business} on {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. See you soon!

Hey {first name}, {provider} is looking forward to seeing you {day} at {time}. Reply C to confirm. {business}

Hi {first name}, this is {business}. You're booked for {service} on {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or call {phone} to change. Thanks!

Quick reminder from {business}: {service} with {provider}, {day} at {time}. We're at {address}. Reply C to confirm.

{first name}, your {service} is tomorrow at {time}! Reply C to confirm or R if you need to reschedule. {business}

Professional and Clinical

This is a reminder of your appointment at {practice} on {date} at {time}. Please arrive 10 minutes early. Reply C to confirm or call {phone} to reschedule.

{practice}: Your appointment with Dr. {last name} is scheduled for {date} at {time}. Please reply C to confirm. 24-hour notice is required to cancel without a fee.

Reminder: {service} at {practice}, {date} {time}. Bring your insurance card and ID. Reply C to confirm.

Your appointment at {practice} is {day} at {time}. Reply C to confirm, R to reschedule, or STOP to opt out of reminders.

Confirmation Texts

You're confirmed! {service} with {business} on {date} at {time}. Add it to your calendar: {link}

Thanks {first name}, we've got you down for {date} at {time}. Reply R anytime to reschedule. {business}

Booking confirmed: {service}, {date}, {time}, {address}. Questions? Call {phone}. {business}

Missed Appointment Follow-Ups

Hi {first name}, we missed you today at {time}. No worries, life happens! Tap here to rebook: {link}. {business}

{first name}, you had an appointment with us today at {time} that we didn't see you for. Please call {phone} to reschedule. {business}

We're sorry we missed you today, {first name}. Our policy includes a {fee} no-show fee, which has been applied. Reply or call {phone} with any questions.

Reschedule Requests

Hi {first name}, we need to move your {date} appointment. Could you do {alt date} at {alt time}? Reply Y or call {phone}. Sorry for the hassle! {business}

{first name}, {provider} is out on {date}. We've held {alt date} at {alt time} for you; reply Y to take it. {business}

Win-Back Texts

Hi {first name}, it's been a while! {business} would love to see you again. Book here: {link}

{first name}, you're due for your {service}. Grab a time that works: {link}. {business}

Day-Of Texts

See you today at {time}, {first name}! Running late? Text us at {phone}. {business}

Today's the day! {service} at {time}. Park in the lot behind the building. {business}

Tips for Writing Your Own

Put the date and time in the first sentence so it survives the lock-screen preview. Say who you are; a text from an unknown number with no business name gets deleted. Ask for a single-letter reply so confirming takes one tap. Mention your cancellation window once, not in every message. Send the reminder when people are planning, not when they are busy: most Slotwise customers send 24 hours before and a second nudge two hours before, and that pairing confirms about 80 percent of bookings.

Finally, keep it legal. Reminder texts are transactional, but you still need the client's number to have been given to you for this purpose, and every message should honor a STOP reply. Slotwise handles opt-outs automatically.
