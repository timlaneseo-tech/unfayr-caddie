How to Stop Double Booking Appointments

Double booking is when two appointments are scheduled for the same person at the same time. It happens when bookings come from more than one place and nothing is checking them against each other: the phone, a paper book, Instagram DMs, and a booking page all writing to different calendars.

Why It Happens

Three causes account for nearly all of it. Multiple calendars that do not sync. Appointments with no buffer, so a service that runs long overlaps the next. And staff who book from memory rather than from the calendar.

How to Prevent It

Use one calendar as the source of truth and make every other channel write to it. Turn on two-way sync between your booking tool and your personal calendar so a dentist appointment you add on your phone blocks the slot. Add buffers between appointments. If you have staff, give each their own calendar and let the booking page show only their real availability.

Google Calendar and Double Booking

Google Calendar will happily let you create overlapping events; it was built for meetings, where overlaps are normal. If you use an appointment schedule, it does check for conflicts on the calendars you select, but only those calendars, which is why a booking still slips through when a staff member keeps a separate one.

When It Happens Anyway

Call both clients immediately, keep the one who booked first, and give the other the next available slot plus something for the trouble. A short, direct apology works better than an elaborate one: "I double booked your appointment and I'm sorry. I can see you at 3pm today or 10am tomorrow, and your next visit is on us."
