Calendly vs Acuity: Which Should You Pick?

Calendly and Acuity Scheduling are the two names most people know, and they are built for different people. Calendly is for booking meetings. Acuity is for booking paid services. Pick the one that matches what you sell and you will be happy; pick the other and you will spend months fighting it.

Who Each Is For

Calendly grew up in sales and recruiting: sharing a link so someone can grab a 30-minute call. It is excellent at that. Acuity, now owned by Squarespace, grew up with service businesses: intake forms, packages, gift certificates, deposits, and a client-facing scheduler with your branding on it.

Pricing

Calendly has a free tier with one event type and paid plans from around $10 per seat per month. Acuity starts at about $16 a month for one calendar with no free tier beyond a trial, and climbs with the number of staff calendars. For a solo service provider the two end up close; for a team booking internal meetings Calendly is cheaper, and for a multi-stylist salon Acuity's per-calendar pricing adds up.

Payments and Deposits

Acuity takes payments and deposits natively through Stripe, Square, or PayPal on its paid plans. Calendly added payments later and they work through Stripe or PayPal on its higher tiers, but there is no deposit-and-balance flow, which service businesses need.

Reminders

Both send email reminders. Text reminders are a paid add-on in both, and Acuity's are more flexible about timing and content.

For Therapists and Salons

Therapists usually land on Acuity for the intake forms and HIPAA option on the top plan. Salons also lean Acuity for the service menu and deposits, though many outgrow it toward a salon-specific tool once they have several chairs.

Our Honest Take

If you book meetings, Calendly. If you book paid appointments, Acuity, or a tool like Slotwise that was built for small service businesses from the start and includes text reminders and deposits on a $19 plan. We would rather you choose well than choose us.
