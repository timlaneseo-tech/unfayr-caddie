Online Booking for Salons

Half of salon bookings now happen outside business hours. If the only way to book with you is to call during the day, half your potential clients are booking with someone else at 10pm from their couch. Online booking fixes that, and it also quietly fixes the no-shows, the double bookings, and the hours a week someone spends on the phone.

What Salon Owners Worry About

Losing control of the calendar, clients booking the wrong service or stylist, and the cost. All three are solvable. Good booking tools let you set which services each stylist offers, how long each takes including processing time, and buffers between appointments. Many have free plans that cover a solo stylist or a small shop.

Features That Matter for Salons

Service menus with durations and prices. Stylist-level calendars. Processing time for color so the chair is not blocked while a client develops. Deposits for long or new-client appointments. Automatic reminder texts. A booking page that works on a phone, because that is where almost all salon bookings start. Instagram and Google Business Profile integration so "Book" buttons point somewhere.

Getting Set Up

Start by listing every service with a realistic duration; most salons underestimate. Add each stylist and their hours. Decide your cancellation policy and whether new clients pay a deposit. Connect your booking link to your Instagram bio, your Google Business Profile, and your website. Then tell existing clients: a text saying "You can now book online any time at this link" converts most of them in a month.

Getting Clients to Use It

Put the link everywhere a client might look, make the first online booking easy, and have the front desk offer to book the next appointment online at checkout so the client sees how it works. Within a quarter most salons see 70 percent or more of bookings arrive online.

What It Costs

Salon booking software ranges from free to around $100 a month per location. The free tiers usually limit bookings or staff; paid tiers add payments, marketing, and reporting. Slotwise is free for up to 50 bookings a month and $19 a month per location after that.
