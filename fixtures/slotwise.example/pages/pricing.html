Simple pricing

Two plans. Start free, upgrade when you outgrow it, cancel any time.

Free

$0. Up to 50 bookings a month. One staff member. Booking page, reminder texts and emails, Google Calendar sync, cancellation policy with checkbox.

Pro

$19 a month per location. Unlimited bookings and staff. Everything in Free plus deposits and card-on-file, no-show fee charging, waitlist, reporting, Outlook sync, and priority support.

Deposits and payments

On Pro you can require a deposit for any service, hold a card on file, and charge a no-show or late cancellation fee with one click. Payments are processed by Stripe at 2.9% plus 30 cents per transaction; Slotwise adds no extra fee.

Questions

Is there a contract? No. Can I switch plans? Any time, prorated. Do you charge per text? No, reminder texts are included on both plans in the US and Canada.
