What Is a Booking Page?

A booking page is a single web page where a client chooses a service, picks an available time, enters their details, and confirms, with no back-and-forth. It is the online equivalent of the appointment book at the front desk, except it is open all night and it never double books.

How It Works

The page reads your calendar, shows only the times that are genuinely free, and when a client picks one it writes the appointment back to your calendar and sends a confirmation. Most booking pages live at a short link you can share anywhere: your Instagram bio, your Google Business Profile, your email signature, a QR code on the counter.

What to Put on It

Your services with durations and prices, so clients pick the right one. Your cancellation policy, with a checkbox. A short line about where you are and how to find you. Your logo and a photo; people want to see who they are booking with. Nothing else. The best booking pages are short.

Booking Page vs. Website

A website explains your business. A booking page takes the booking. Many small businesses run perfectly well with only a booking page and a Google Business Profile, and add a website later if they want one. If you already have a website, the booking page is where its "Book now" button should point.

How to Make One

Sign up for a scheduling tool, add your services and hours, connect your calendar, and copy the link. In Slotwise that takes about ten minutes and the page is free. Then put the link in your Instagram bio and on your Google Business Profile, where most of your bookings will come from.
